# Detroit reference plan — 2026-10-23

Research scope: one recognizable Hart Plaza/Riverwalk slice first, then the civic-building facade and Renaissance Center podium. This is a reference brief for original game geometry, not survey or texture/model licensing. Existing source-map footprints, shoreline, landmarks and gameplay anchors remain authoritative until reviewed by the Lead.

## First build slice

Improve the Hart Plaza-to-Riverwalk transition with a broad upper paved landing, stepped lower terraces and an adjacent continuous accessible route. Group stairs, landings, retaining edges, planting and railings into one independently replaceable detail model. Keep the existing river edge, fountain/statue anchors and transit/extraction paths. Use existing projection and coordinate data; do not invent a different map scale or hills across downtown.

Initial dimensions are **gameplay estimates**, not real-world measurements: the City sources establish the relationship and program, but the available search text did not expose readable survey elevations. Avoid calling the result a faithful 2026 as-built reconstruction. Keep reference-inspired terraces opt-in until collision/route and foreground FPS checks pass. A ramp must actually connect upper and lower landing surfaces and remain traversable at supported player sizes.

## Immediate civic facade handoff

The [Michigan Modern building entry](https://www.michiganmodern.org/modern-buildings/city-county-building-coleman-a-young-municipal-center/) explicitly credits its text to the City of Detroit Historic Designation Advisory Board walking-tour script. It distinguishes the **20-story vertical Courts Tower** from the **14-story horizontal Administration Tower**. River-facing and rear Courts elevations have full-height marble strips separating repeated window bays; Administration front/back use repeated **four-unit ribbon-window groups**. The connecting link is glass, with a long yellow metal entrance canopy. The Woodward side is blank marble behind the Spirit statue; the east side is brick with two ribbon-window groups per floor, offset northward. Do not distribute identical windows around every face. These descriptions establish rhythm, not measured bay widths. Keep existing 97 m / 60 m OSM masses pending Lead placement review; the floor-height difference is not evidence to rescale either footprint.

Additional primary photographic references discovered: [facade engineering consultant Walter P Moore](https://www.walterpmoore.com/projects/coleman-a-young-municipal-center-enclosure-consulting), [property manager Hines](https://www.hines.com/properties/coleman-a.-young-municipal-building-detroit). The image search found their facade photographs, but exact photographed bay counts were not visually measured. Original procedural framing/materials only; no photo texture import.
