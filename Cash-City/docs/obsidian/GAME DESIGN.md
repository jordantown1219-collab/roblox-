# Cash City — approved game design

Multiplayer Roblox game in realistic U.S. city environments, starting with Detroit's riverfront. Current work remains Map 1: Renaissance Center, Wintergarden, RiverWalk and park. The apartment interiors are an original fictional residential conversion, not an as-built architectural claim.

- Each player shrinks independently through 100%, 65%, 40%, 22% gate stages.
- Strength, health, armor and weapon damage scale according to the established gameplay design.
- The whole team must finish to extract. Do not replace this with synchronized team shrinking.
- Planned team sizes: 3v3, 4v4, 5v5.
- Planned overall map categories: two large, two medium, one small experimental; additional districts/maps are deferred from the current release focus.
- Platforms: PC, mobile, tablet and supported Roblox consoles; labels and controls must be usable on each.
- Lobby/settings voting and additional modes are planned work, not permission to resume them while the current focus is Map 1. Extraction remains the core mode.
- Realistic proportions/materials and recognizable city layout, fictional businesses only, original artwork, no copied logos/sports teams/real people.
- Preserve working doors/windows, shrink-accessible navigation and completed landmarks. Drivable vehicles and other unfinished systems must not be reported complete without evidence.
- Never publish without the owner's approval.
