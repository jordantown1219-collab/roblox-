**Riverfront authority: [Detroit Riverfront Conservancy RiverWalk page](https://www.detroitriverfront.org/plan-your-visit/parks-greenways/the-riverwalk).** Actual hero image viewed: pale concrete trail with joints, a low retaining edge/metal railing, rock revetment and grassy slope. It depicts another trail segment and must not be transplanted literally to the RenCen plaza. The authority identifies the Renaissance Center-to-Cullen Plaza path as the first RiverWalk segment. Preserve continuous pedestrian/bicycle circulation rather than making the entire waterfront a wooden pier. The page mixes newer overview text and older FAQ entries; avoid deriving exact current system length from it.

## Immediate builder instructions

- Tower accents: thin silver-gray upper bands and sparse vertical mullions are consistent with the viewed facade. Retain dark blue/teal glazing; avoid bright opaque window grids or a new neon skyline. Exact bay widths/spacing were not measured.
- Podium: original low glass-and-pale-panel bands; place any curved Wintergarden-inspired feature on the river-facing side only after Lead verifies its existing footprint and orientation. Do not clone the street entrance into the waterfront.
- Riverfront: **paved promenade**, using muted gray rectangular joint rhythm and broad uninterrupted walking strips. Timber benches/deck accents can be artistic additions but must not be described as an authentic all-timber Detroit boardwalk.
- The helper's bounded 80 m long / 2 m wide overlay is a gameplay detail strip inside an existing path, not a measurement of the real plaza or complete promenade width. Keep elevation offsets small and aligned to existing ground, with no coplanar surfaces or raised trip edges.
- Add props in clusters: benches, a few slim metal poles and small planting pockets, leaving the path clear at all supported sizes. No repeated active scripts per paver or rail segment. Any light emission must be restrained and performance checked.
